Mudanças (era FSP)
During the FMLN era (2009–2018) the Constitutional Chamber was notably independent, ruling against both ARENA and FMLN governments. The decisive change came AFTER the FSP era: on 1 May 2021 President Bukele’s Assembly (Nuevas Ideas) removed the 5 Constitutional Chamber magistrates and the Attorney General, replacing them with loyalists; in Sept 2021 the new chamber authorised presidential re-election. This was done by Bukele, not by an FSP president.
Quanto permanece
The pre-2021 Constitutional Chamber was entirely removed; the Bukele-aligned magistrates installed on 1 May 2021 remain in place.

2009–2021 partial bench of 5 Bench tracked here = the 5-member Constitutional Chamber ('Sala de lo Constitucional', 5 of the 15 CSJ magistrates), the constitutionally decisive body. During the FMLN governments (2009–2019) magistrates were elected by a two-thirds Assembly vote through the long-standing ARENA–FMLN power-sharing — the FMLN caucus co-selected 2 of the 4 seats filled in the 2009 cycle, never a chamber majority on its own. The resulting 'Sala 4' (2009–2018) became known for landmark rulings against both the ARENA and FMLN governments — an appointment-provenance-without-alignment case, not FSP control. The successor chamber was removed en masse on 1 May 2021 by Bukele's new (non-FSP) Assembly (see courtHistory), ending the FMLN-era co-appointed presence. (fonte 1, fonte 2, fonte 3)
