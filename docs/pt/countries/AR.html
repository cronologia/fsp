Nomeados sob governos FSP
1 of 3 sitting justices — a minority of the current bench. This is appointment provenance (who nominated each justice), not a claim about how they rule — justices serve independently.
Mudanças (era FSP)
In 1990 President Menem (Peronist, pre-Kirchner) expanded the court from 5 to 9 (Law 23.774), creating an "automatic majority". In 2006 the Kirchnerist government reduced it back to 5 (Law 26.183) and had tightened the appointment process (Decree 222/03, 2003). So the packing predates the Kirchnerist (FSP-affiliated) governments, which actually de-packed it — a useful counter to a simplistic 'FSP packs courts' reading.
Quanto permanece
After the retirements of Highton (2021) and Maqueda (2024), the court fell below its 5-seat complement to 3 justices; President Milei's attempts to fill the seats by decree (Lijo, García-Mansilla) failed in the Senate (2025), leaving a deadlock as of 2026.

Before 1990 (context) composition Earlier history: the Corte Suprema was installed in 1863 (5 justices). In 1947 Perón's Congress removed 4 of the 5 justices; the court was expanded 5→7 (1958) and returned to 5 (1966); the 1976–83 junta replaced it.
