7 of 11 sitting justices — a majority of the current bench. This is appointment provenance (who nominated each justice), not a claim about how they rule — justices serve independently.
Mudanças (era FSP)
No structural change to the court. PT presidents (Lula, Dilma) filled vacancies through the normal process. As of 2026 a majority of the 11 sitting justices were appointed by PT (FSP) presidents: Lula appointed Cármen Lúcia, Dias Toffoli, Cristiano Zanin and Flávio Dino (with Jorge Messias nominated for the seat vacated by Barroso's 2025 retirement); Dilma appointed Luiz Fux and Edson Fachin (the current court president). The rest were appointed by FHC (Gilmar Mendes), Temer (Alexandre de Moraes) and Bolsonaro (Nunes Marques, André Mendonça).

1990–2002 independent 0 of 11 All 11 justices were appointees of pre-FSP-era presidents (Sarney through Cardoso); the PT first won the presidency in the 2002 election. (fonte 1)
2003–2005 partial bench of 11 Lula's first appointments — Cezar Peluso, Ayres Britto and Joaquim Barbosa (2003), then Eros Grau (2004) — put 3–4 PT-era appointees on the 11-seat bench. (fonte 1)
2006–2010 aligned bench of 11 With Ricardo Lewandowski and Cármen Lúcia (2006) the tally reached 6 of 11 — a majority by appointment provenance — and 7 with Menezes Direito (2007; Dias Toffoli took that seat in 2009). Provenance did not dictate outcomes: Lula appointee Joaquim Barbosa went on to lead the mensalão corruption convictions of senior PT figures. (fonte 1, fonte 2)
2011–2016 aligned bench of 11 Dilma Rousseff's appointments (Fux and Rosa Weber 2011, Zavascki 2012, Barroso 2013, Fachin 2015) kept 7–8 of the 11 seats held by Lula/Dilma appointees — the peak, 8 of 11, around 2015–16. (fonte 1, fonte 2)
2017–2022 aligned 7 of 11 7 of the 11 sitting justices were Lula/Dilma appointees (MercoPress, 2018); the period's vacancies went to Temer (Moraes 2017) and Bolsonaro (Nunes Marques 2020, Mendonça 2021). The same court upheld Lula's imprisonment in 2018 — appointment provenance is not a voting bloc. (fonte 1, fonte 2)
2023–present aligned bench of 11 Lula's third-term appointments (Zanin 2023, Dino 2024) offset PT-era retirements (Lewandowski and Rosa Weber, 2023); 6–7 of 11 as of 2026, with Jorge Messias's confirmation pending for Barroso's seat after his October 2025 retirement. (fonte 1, fonte 2)
