FSP member parties (highlighted FSP): Partido Nacionalista Peruano (PNP), Perú Libre (PL). Peru has two documented FSP members. The Partido Nacionalista Peruano (Ollanta Humala) appears on Regalado's 2008 roster and led the Gana Perú alliance to power in 2011. Perú Libre (Vladimir Cerrón) joined the Forum as Perú Libertario at the XXIV Encuentro (Havana 2018) and announced its formal incorporation as a full member on 30 June 2020; the Forum publicly defended Cerrón against what it called political persecution in 2021. Perú Libre won the 2021 election with Pedro Castillo and, after the interim churn of 2025–26, provided the congressionally elected interim president José María Balcázar (Feb 2026).

Government: Ollanta Humala (PNP / Gana Perú) — lacked a majority. FSP party in government: yes. Gana Perú was the largest bloc (47/130) but well short of a 66-seat majority. Humala assembled a working majority with Alejandro Toledo's Perú Posible (21 seats); that partnership was fragile and the government's congressional support fluctuated over the term. Government: Pedro Castillo (Perú Libre) — lacked a majority. FSP party in government: yes. Castillo faced two impeachment attempts before Congress removed him in December 2022 after he tried to dissolve it; his own caucus fragmented along the way. (fuente 1, fuente 2)
