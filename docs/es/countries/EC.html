FSP member parties (highlighted FSP): Alianza PAIS / Revolución Ciudadana (Correísmo), Partido Comunista del Ecuador, Partido Socialista Ecuatoriano. Ecuador's FSP member is Rafael Correa's movement — Alianza PAIS, now Revolución Ciudadana (UNES) — alongside smaller left parties. Important split: Correa's successor Lenín Moreno (2017–2021) broke with Correísmo and turned against it, which is why he is marked non-FSP even though he was elected on the PAIS ticket. Since 2017 the FSP-aligned bloc is Correísmo/RC, in opposition.

Correísmo's Revolución Ciudadana was again the largest single bloc (67) but stayed in opposition; President Noboa's ADN (66) governs as a minority, dependent on Pachakutik, the Social Christians and smaller movements. 2007–2011 partial — Correa's 2008 Constitution replaced the Corte Suprema with the Corte Nacional de Justicia and a new Corte Constitucional; transition benches straddled the old and new systems, and the May 2011 referendum dissolved the old Judicial Council, giving the executive a direct role in its replacement (HRW). Supporters presented the overhaul as reform of a dysfunctional system. (fuente 1, fuente 2)
2019–present independent 0 After the February 2018 referendum under Moreno, a transitory citizen-participation council replaced the Correa-era Judicial Council and fully renewed the Constitutional Court (2019), which observers have since treated as an independent check (HRW, 2021); the correísta movement has been out of government throughout.
