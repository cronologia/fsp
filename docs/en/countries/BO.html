High court — Tribunal Supremo de Justicia / Tribunal Constitucional Plurinacional

Appointment
Since the 2009 Constitution, high-court judges are chosen by popular election from candidates pre-selected by the (MAS-controlled) Legislative Assembly.
Changes (FSP era)
The 2009 Constitution introduced popular election of top judges (held 2011, 2017). In Dec 2023 the Constitutional Tribunal extended ("self-prorogued") magistrates' mandates after elections were delayed — widely criticised. Partial judicial elections were held in late 2024.
How much remains
Composition in flux through 2024–2025; the 2023 self-extended magistrates and the partial 2024-elected judges sit under the new (post-MAS) government — full roster to verify.
