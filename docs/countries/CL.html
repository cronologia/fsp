High court — Corte Suprema de Chile

Seats
21
Appointment
Justices proposed by the Court itself, chosen by the President, and confirmed by 2/3 of the Senate.
Changes (FSP era)
No court-packing by left governments. Judicial appointments followed the regular mechanism. Two constituent processes (2021–2023) to replace the 1980 constitution both failed at referendum, leaving the judiciary’s structure unchanged.
How much remains
Court independent; no FSP-driven structural change.
