FSP member parties (highlighted FSP): Partido Nacionalista Peruano (PNP). Peru's FSP member is the Partido Nacionalista Peruano (Ollanta Humala), per Regalado's 2008 roster. In 2011 the PNP led the Gana Perú electoral alliance (which also included smaller left parties). The PNP has had no congressional presence since Humala left office in 2016.

FSP member parties together: 47 of 130 (36%) — 19 seats short of the 66-seat majority. The FSP party held the presidency but not a majority in this chamber.

Government: Ollanta Humala (PNP / Gana Perú) — lacked a majority. FSP party in government: yes. Gana Perú was the largest bloc (47/130) but well short of a 66-seat majority. Humala assembled a working majority with Alejandro Toledo's Perú Posible (21 seats); that partnership was fragile and the government's congressional support fluctuated over the term.
