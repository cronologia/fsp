FSP member parties (highlighted FSP): Libertad y Refundación (LIBRE). LIBRE, formed in 2011 by supporters of the ousted Manuel Zelaya, is Honduras's FSP member. Before 2011 there was no FSP party in Congress.

FSP member parties together: 50 of 128 (39%) — 15 seats short of the 65-seat majority. The FSP party held the presidency but not a majority in this chamber.

Government: Xiomara Castro (LIBRE) — lacked a majority. FSP party in government: yes. LIBRE won a plurality (50/128); even with its electoral ally PSH (10) the government bloc held 60 seats — 5 short of a majority. The gap showed immediately: in January 2022 a group of LIBRE dissidents backed a rival congressional leadership, splitting the chamber before a negotiated settlement. Castro governed without reliable control of Congress. (source 1, source 2)

Before the FSP era: in Dec 2012 Congress removed four Constitutional Chamber justices ("technical coup"), and in 2015 the court lifted the ban on presidential re-election (enabling Hernández). Under Castro/LIBRE, Congress elected a new 15-magistrate court in Feb 2023.
