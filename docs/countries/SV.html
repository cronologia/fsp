FSP member parties together: 35 of 84 (42%) — 8 seats short of the 43-seat majority. The FSP party held the presidency but not a majority in this chamber.

Government: Mauricio Funes (FMLN) — lacked a majority. FSP party in government: yes. The FMLN's best-ever result — the largest bloc, but 8 seats short of a majority. The Funes government legislated through shifting deals with the smaller right-of-centre parties (and, from late 2009, the GANA split from ARENA); the FMLN never controlled the Assembly outright in either of its two presidencies (2009–2019). Appointment
Magistrates elected by the Legislative Assembly.
Changes (FSP era)
During the FMLN era (2009–2018) the Constitutional Chamber was notably independent, ruling against both ARENA and FMLN governments. The decisive change came AFTER the FSP era: on 1 May 2021 President Bukele’s Assembly (Nuevas Ideas) removed the 5 Constitutional Chamber magistrates and the Attorney General, replacing them with loyalists; in Sept 2021 the new chamber authorised presidential re-election. This was done by Bukele, not by an FSP president.
How much remains
The pre-2021 Constitutional Chamber was entirely removed; the Bukele-aligned magistrates installed on 1 May 2021 remain in place.
