FSP member parties (highlighted FSP): Frente Amplio (FA). The Frente Amplio is itself a coalition of FSP-listed factions — Movimiento de Participación Popular, Partido Socialista, Partido Comunista del Uruguay, Asamblea Uruguay, MLN-Tupamaros and others (Regalado 2007). It contests elections as a single bloc, so its seats are the FSP bloc.

No court-packing. The court remained independent during FA governments — notably its 2013 ruling striking down parts of a law on prosecution of past (dictatorship-era) crimes ran against the FA government’s position.
