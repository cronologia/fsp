Changes (FSP era)
The judiciary has been consolidated under FSLN control. In 2009 the CSJ Constitutional Chamber set aside the constitutional ban on consecutive presidential re-election, enabling Ortega’s continued rule; later codified by constitutional reform (2014).
How much remains
The court remains under FSLN control; after the 2009 ruling and 2014 constitutional reform there is no independent bloc, and the judiciary has been central to Ortega and Murillo's consolidation of power. Exact 2026 roster not individually itemised here.
