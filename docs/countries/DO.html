FSP member parties (highlighted FSP): Partido de la Liberación Dominicana (PLD). The Dominican Republic's FSP member is the Partido de la Liberación Dominicana (PLD), founded by Juan Bosch and listed on Regalado's 2008 roster. In government the PLD governed as a broadly centrist, pro-business party — its FSP affiliation is documented but its policy record is not straightforwardly left. It held the presidency 1996–2000 and 2004–2020 and lost power in 2020.

2020 — Cámara de Diputados (190 seats; majority = 96)

FSP member parties together: 75 of 190 (39%) — 21 seats short of the 96-seat majority. The FSP parties sat in opposition.

Government: Luis Abinader (PRM) — commanded a working majority. FSP party in government: no. The end of 16 years of PLD rule: the FSP member fell from 106 to 75 seats and lost the presidency. The PLD had also split — Leonel Fernández's breakaway Fuerza del Pueblo drew away part of its vote. The PRM alone (86 of 190) fell just short of an absolute majority, but with allied minor parties the incoming Abinader government controlled the chamber (per CSIS) and swept most Senate races. (source 1, source 2, source 3)

Government: Danilo Medina (PLD) — commanded a working majority. FSP party in government: yes. The PLD won an outright majority (106/190) in its own right and, with allied parties in its 'Bloque Progresista', controlled well over two-thirds of the chamber. (source 1, source 2)
