Appointment
Nominated by the President, confirmed by 2/3 of the Senate.
Changes (FSP era)
In 1990 President Menem (Peronist, pre-Kirchner) expanded the court from 5 to 9, creating an "automatic majority". In 2006 Néstor Kirchner reduced it back to 5 and tightened the appointment process (Decree 222/03 increasing transparency). The packing thus predates the Kirchnerist (FSP-affiliated) governments, which partly reversed it.
